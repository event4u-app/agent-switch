## Sound familiar?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="“I keep logging out and back in.”" icon="puzzle">
    Every account stays signed in at the same time. Switching never logs anyone out.
  </Card>
  <Card title="“One account runs dry, the other resets unused.”" icon="warning">
    Each new session starts on the account that would otherwise waste the most of its quota at its next reset.
  </Card>
  <Card title="“I never know what is left.”" icon="information">
    The desktop app shows every account's 5-hour and weekly limit, live, in your menu bar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="“Client work ends up on my personal account.”" icon="setting">
    Pin a folder to an account once. Every session started there uses it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## See it decide

Two accounts are signed in. `personal` still has 66% of its week left and
resets in 14 hours. `work` has 19% left over five days. A new session goes to
`personal`, because its quota would expire first. A client folder is pinned to
`work`.

![Terminal demo: a new session goes to the account whose quota would expire first; a client folder is pinned to the work account](/agent-switch/media/demo.gif)

## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Never log in twice" icon="approve-check">
    Each account has its own folder and its own login, the way the provider
    intended. Agent Switch only decides which folder the next session uses. It
    never copies or swaps a login.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never waste a paid limit" icon="rocket">
    New sessions are balanced across your accounts by what each would lose at
    its next reset. Accounts at their limit are skipped. `dir --json` tells you
    why.
  </Card>
  <Card title="See everything" icon="laptop">
    One window and a menu-bar popover: every account's limits, every running
    session with its context %, and the one thing that needs you next.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Move anything" icon="random">
    Move a session to another account and continue it there. On macOS, switch
    the account of a session that is still running, now or when its turn ends.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

![The Agent Switch desktop app: what needs you, what is running, and what every account has left](/agent-switch/media/screenshots/overview.png)

<Aside type="tip" title="Your logins stay yours">
  Agent Switch is not a proxy and has no server, no account and no telemetry.
  Logins stay where the provider put them. Nothing switches a running session
  without your confirmation.
  [What it reads, stores and sends](/agent-switch/trust/security-and-privacy/).
</Aside>
